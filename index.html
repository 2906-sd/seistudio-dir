Hi! I'm a design student (ex-STEM) and the self-taught coder behind Sei Out Loud.

Growing up under strict parents (who I still live with, arghh), I never got to fully experience being on the internet in the early 2000s-2010s. Now that I'm an adult, I'm slowly giving myself that experience again. I was getting tired of mainstream social media algorithms, so towards the end of 2024, I decided to build my own catch-all space on the web. I first learned web design in community college using Glitch several years ago (RIP to my early projects), but building this site has rekindled my love for code, especially now that my design studies overlap with it.

I’m still learning and relearning as I go, backed by dev friends, a systems engineer dad, and endless inspiration from the Neocities community.

Originally this site was just going to be for myself, my art and my writing, but I started making stuff for other things on it too once I made the decision to make a space here on Neocities and wanted to just…be out there. It's taken many forms, and it'll probably continue to change!

So here I am, Sei, out loud. :)

Here you’ll find multimedia art, fan art, silly digital shrines, blog posts of all kinds of manners, and other things you might find interesting. Eventually I'll add more spaces on here for my other interests (see below) and other things, I hope you'll stick around to see them!

When I’m not tinkering here (or changing the theme for the 100th time), I’m studying, working, drowning in hobbies, or reading fanfic.

Feel free to reach out and say hi! I'm a little slow to reply, but I always answer. :)

Fangirling

A dossier on my identity as a fangirl, her appetite, and favs.

FANNISH BEHAVIOR

Let me preface that I’ve never written my stance out loud! I learned about flavor profiles from a friend only recently and am slowly having a better understanding of who I am as a fangirl.

I’m someone who mostly exists at the margins of fandoms. Having had restricted internet and media access growing up, I learned early on to observe and consume things from the margins, a habit I never really unlearned. I engage by reading fanfiction, crafting OCs for canon universes, and occasionally drawing fanart (which I mostly keep to myself, with the exception of Sei Out Loud). I genuinely love being part of the audience that witnesses everyone else’s passionate contributions, so I'm content to lurk and appreciate the energy from afar.

But I’m much more of a listener. While I enjoy sharing fandom spaces with friends, I rarely feel the need for intense, deep debates—mostly because I don’t always have the energy to match that level of enthusiasm. I’m always happy to hear people talk about their favs, but outside my immediate circle, I tend to be quite self-contained. Long-time friends will occasionally get random hyperfixation texts or shared fanart for a quick serotonin boost (or as I like to put it, "getting the fangirl zoomies out") but overall, my behavior is just shyness and low energy, never hostility!

Outwardly, I process fandom in small increments, while inwardly keeping that intoxicating, obsessive excitement for myself. When I do decide to express that energy here on my blog, I do so with zero pressure on anyone reading—feel free to consume it, react if you’d like, and move on at your own pace.

in detail

Shipping and romance, yes and yes, but I’m mostly a monoshipper. I usually like romance when there’s a source of tension or conflict beyond the usual suspects (I’m such a sucker for fake dating or marriage of convenience tropes) so for me it comes down to the set up more than the pay off, and I won’t consume anything where the character dynamics do the heavy lifting - I want decent plot and worldbuilding.

I also love angst, especially if its an enemies to lovers trope. I like it best when the antagonism doesn’t fully resolve and there’s still a little bite left after they fall in love. Extra bonus if there’s slow burn. I’m not too into fluff, I’m not really into stuff that’s overly sweet and cutesy, but I’ll make an exception for lovesick men and himbos. IDK, I get fond of those guys, lol. Relationships that seem wrong are stuff I don’t mind, love me some older partners so long as the other character is a young adult and uneven dynamics where someone has to cede ground they didn't expect to. Don’t really have a preference for configuration, so HL/BL/GL are all fair game. I will say that I mostly like HL and GL. I use one ship name regardless of who is top/bottom, just for the sake of simplicity.

HOWEVER I can’t stand when the girl is really submissive in HL or GL, especially if her partner is toxic/violent and she continues to be with them. I like it when a girl is assertive or stubborn - or when a girl isn’t afraid to challenge or claim equal footing and her partner is like, stupidly into that. Big fan of girl pairings where both sides are, on the surface, not the kind who would romantically involve themselves with the other by societal standards (i.e. preppy girl x goth girl). 

I also read self-inserts. Shameless plug. Let me in there, damn! (The above applies, of course.) 

I don’t usually seek out media that is considered transgressive. While I value media that provokes discussion and debate, I reject transgressive material because to me it often exposes an individual’s true desires, and I won’t give it a second glance even if the plot is good enough to compensate.

HOW_IT_WORKS

How does this site actually work?

The main version of this site runs on Vercel and has a hidden (not really) admin panel built right in. That means I can publish and edit content without deep diving too much into lines of code. (read: you're always going to be drowning in code, lol.) I actually made this site originally for Vercel before giving it a second home here on neocities!

All content — art, logs, letters, shrines, even the bulletin board messages — lives in a database called Firebase, which keeps it stored and synced safely. The site's code itself is version-controlled on GitHub, but I mostly upload content via Vercel. Whenever I publish something new, an automated workflow kicks in and mirrors the update over to Neocities as a static copy, so both versions stay in sync without any manual work on my end.

Heads up: if you're viewing this on Neocities and try to log into the admin panel, it won't work there. :p Neocities only hosts a static snapshot of the site, so the login and publishing tools only run on the originsl Vercel deployment.

WHY_I_MADE_THIS

So why does this site even exist?

For all intents and purposes, Sei Out Loud is a corner on the web that I coded out of spite and anger, in retaliation against Big Algorithm! This is a place where I can say and show things without worrying if people will enjoy it enough to give it a like, a comment or a repost. I barely experienced that era of the Internet when I was wee child with parents who did, unfortunately, give a damn about what their kid was looking at on the family computer. (And look where that got me, HA.)

Everything on Sei Out Loud is here because I thought it looked cool or held personal value to me. The site functions as a catch all place for everything that I like, a repository of my mind, made out of free will. And you, Intrepid Neocities Wanderer, are here out of free will too! There will be no AI or data stealing or any of that corporate tech evildoing. I am here to be unapologetic, loud, and be unorganized with my horrendous code.

When I first started building this place in 2024, I had originally wanted a minimalist color palette to the point where it looked a little brutalist. It was also originally named Sei Studio. I was really inspired by web designs that emulated the Apple OS 1 look or mimicked a desktop environment. I'm talking stark contrast, mostly monotone color palette with like a hint of color. I did this because originally I was just going to post my art and have a digital journal for friends to see since I wanted to step away from social media. I didn't want anything to clash with my art, but I settled with blue and red since those are also some of my favorite colors. I guess in my mind I wanted a simple look in case I wanted to use the site as a professional portfolio. I figured out how to host it on Vercel, and that was that.

The moodboard for Sei Out Loud that I made. At the time of writing this I can't find the poorly sketched out site plans I had, so this will have to suffice. Can you tell I'm not very good at moodboarding or planning? hehe.

It stayed like that for some time. Every once in a while I'd try and rearrange some stuff, but it was a fairly small site, and I was (still am) busy with classes and work so I actually didn't upload stuff on there as often as I imagined to. But it had a gallery, a blog space, and the bulletin board had its own page. I shared the link to my friends and colleagues and let them look around, it looked really cool with all their messages in the board. Eventually I started shifting away from a simple portfolio-esque site and started personalizing it a little more as I kept finding web development sources and relearning coding. I hadn't coded anything since taking some web development courses a few years prior, and coding languages are always evolving, so I had a lot of catching up to do!

The color scheme didn't really change until earlier this year actually, and it was because I wanted to put my site on neocities! When I found out about neocities in 2025 I remember browsing people's sites and thinking how awesome it was that there's this indie web community and how creative people were with making their own sites. There was a moment where I thought my site was so underwhelming, but I quickly had to tell myself that my coding knowledge is still rather beginner at best.

Still, I was really inspired by what people were doing, and it made me realize I was kind of restraining myself (bad habit.) I was seeing people's shrines, collections, toyboxes and the child version of me sort of woke up in the back of my mind and said 'I do that too! I like those things!' It made me realize that this is exactly the kind of space on the web that I would like to inhabit. I suddenly wanted this site to be a true extension of myself and my interests, so Sei Studio became Sei Out Loud.
