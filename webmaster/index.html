Hi! I'm a design student (ex-STEM) and the self-taught coder behind Sei Out Loud.

Growing up under strict parents (who I still live with, arghh), I never got to fully experience being on the internet in the early 2000s-2010s. Now that I'm an adult, I'm slowly giving myself that experience again. I was getting tired of mainstream social media algorithms, so towards the end of 2024, I decided to build my own catch-all space on the web. I first learned web design in community college using Glitch several years ago (RIP to my early projects), but building this site has rekindled my love for code, especially now that my design studies overlap with it.

I'm still learning and relearning as I go, backed by dev friends, a systems engineer dad, and endless inspiration from the Neocities community.

Originally this site was just going to be for myself, my art and my writing, but I started making stuff for other things on it too once I made the decision to make a space here on Neocities and wanted to just…be out there. It's taken many forms, and it'll probably continue to change!

So here I am, Sei, out loud. :)

Here you'll find multimedia art, fan art, silly digital shrines, blog posts of all kinds of manners, and other things you might find interesting. Eventually I'll add more spaces on here for my other interests (see below) and other things, I hope you'll stick around to see them!

When I'm not tinkering here (or changing the theme for the 100th time), I'm studying, working, drowning in hobbies, or reading fanfic.

Feel free to reach out and say hi! I'm a little slow to reply, but I always answer. :)
