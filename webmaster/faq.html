F.A.Q

common questions that have been asked irl/online, badabing badaboom.

me

▾ Is the beauty mark above your lip real?

✿ Yes, and I think it's really silly that this is the question that I get the most! People thought I drew it on with a sharpie/makeup or mistook it for dirt, and now that I'm an adult, some people think I actually got it tatted on! I didn't always have it, I think it showed up on my face some time during middle school. It's not the only beauty mark I have, so it's genetic.

▾ Which ethnicity do you identify with?

✿ Ehh, neither? I think I identify more with being Filipino than Japanese, mostly thanks to my mom. I'm pretty cut off from my Japanese heritage in comparison, so it's really a matter of exposure and conditioning more than anything. I usually just tell people I'm Wasian.

▾ Do you speak other languages?

✿ I'm only fluent (a generous term, really) in English. I can speak a little Korean. I used to know how to speak Tagalog when I was younger but forgot how to. I've been meaning to learn Japanese though. If you have any language learning tips and resources, I'd love to hear them.

▾ Are you okay with talking to people under 18?

✿ Yes, for general and SFW stuff only (fandoms, coding, site design, etc.)! Any discussions on adult/NSFW topics, themes, or suggestive content are strictly off-limits and will be ignored.

▾ Can we exchange site buttons?

✿ Of course!! If you've put my button on your site, please let me know so I can add yours to mine and follow you. :)

▾ Where is the best place to contact or DM you?

✿ Email! If you don't see my reply, check your spam. I may take a while to respond, so please be patient.

▾ You play video games. Do you play online?

✿ I don't really like online / multiplayer games :( I only have so much time to play video games, and I'd rather not spend it depending on others for enjoyment. I'd say my play style is also pretty slow, I like to take my time! The few times I do play with others is with close friends while we're hanging out.

art

▾ What drawing program / brushes / tablet do you use?

✿ I currently draw on Adobe Photoshop (2025) using a Wacom One 13 drawing tablet.

▾ What sketchbooks do you use?

✿ Right now I'm using the Strathmore 500 Series Mixed Media, and Canson Mixed Media for my usual mixed media stuff, and Leuchtturm1917 for light sketches and doodles.

▾ What pencils/pens/erasers do you use?

✿ I switch between a 0.5 mm Bic and a uniball Kuru Toga Mechanical Pencil for sketches and use a Faber Castell graphic pencil set for the more detailed stuff. I have a mix of Microns and Faber Castell pens for ink-work/outlining. For erasers, I use Pentel Hi-Polymer erasers (I recommend getting a big pack of them~) and the Tombow Mono Zero Erasers, sized 2.5mm square and 2.3mm round.

▾ What do you use for mixed media?

✿ Everything and anything, not going to lie! I have a bunch of acrylic paint from Liquitex, an Artist Loft gouache palette (hate it), a Prismacolor Colored Pencil Set (150 ct) and a bunch of knock-off alcohol markers someone got me as a gift. I'll use anything honestly.

▾ Do you prefer traditional or digital?

✿ Traditional, since that's how I started with learning how to draw! I'm somewhat comfortable with digital, but I haven't quite figured out how to translate my artstyle yet and am still learning what rendering technique works best for me.

▾ What sewing machine do you have?

✿ A Brother CP100x that knows I only know how to hem stuff. (I'm still learning the basics haha)
